## Evidence boundary

Historical full game and Codex-session records are intentionally excluded from
the repository. These checks establish the 0.6.0 protocol/rules baseline,
sanitized regression replay, and publication hygiene. They do not establish
complete Magic rules coverage, a completed four-player review, deck
superiority, or statistical matchup evidence.
